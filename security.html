Security & your data

Your customer list is your business. This page says, in plain words, where your data lives, how it's protected, and the rights you keep — no fine print, no inflated claims. If anything here ever changes, this page changes with it.

Where your data lives

GeneralOS runs on established United States cloud infrastructure: our database is hosted by Supabase in its US West region, and our application runs on Render. Connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS everywhere), and stored data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure providers.

Your company is walled off

Every company's workspace is sealed from every other company's. Access is checked on every single request — a signed-in user from one company cannot see another company's customers, deals, or messages, and our own testing tries exactly that on a regular basis. Inside your workspace, your team has clear roles — Owner, Manager, Employee — and the sensitive areas (money, taxes, team management) are limited to the people you choose.

Passwords & sign-in

Passwords are never stored in plain text — only as strong one-way hashes (bcrypt). New team members must verify a one-time emailed code and set their own password before they can use the account. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited, and sessions expire. Even our own backup files exclude password hashes by design: if a backup ever leaked, it would contain no credentials.

Backups — and proof they restore

A backup that has never been restored is a hope, not a backup. On top of our providers' own backup systems, we pull a full backup of the platform every week and store it off-platform — and we test restoring real data into a fresh workspace. Our first restore drill ran on November 5, 2026, and passed: every record checked came back intact.

Your data is yours

Download everything — contacts, pipelines, deals, quotes, tasks — in one click, at any time, in an open format. No exit fees, no hostage negotiations. Cancel any time, and ask us to hard-delete your workspace and we'll do it. We don't lock you in. We don't have to.

Who else touches the data

We keep the list short, and here it is: Render (application hosting), Supabase (database hosting), Stripe (payment processing — only if you connect payments), and Resend (email delivery — only when email features are enabled for your workspace). That's the whole list. We never sell customer data, and we never use your data to train anything.

Straight talk

We're a young company, and we'd rather tell you that than dress this page up. Our protections above are real and tested by us regularly. Independent third-party security testing is on our roadmap before large-scale rollout, and this page will say so plainly when it's done. If you have a security question — or you run a security review before buying — ask us through the Request Work page and you'll get a straight answer.
