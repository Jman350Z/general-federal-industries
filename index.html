Janitorial and facilities services for California government facilities — done right, by Californians.

General Federal Industries LLC is a California limited liability company providing janitorial and facilities services to government facilities across the state. We were founded on a simple conviction: public buildings deserve dependable care, and Californians deserve the work.

Who We Are

General Federal Industries LLC was formed in California in January 2026 to serve government clients with the reliability they require and the accountability taxpayers expect. Our work is straightforward: keep public facilities clean, safe, and ready for the people who depend on them — every shift, every day.

Our mission reaches beyond the contract. We exist to provide employment opportunities across California — steady, honest work in the communities where our clients operate.

What We Do

Janitorial Services
