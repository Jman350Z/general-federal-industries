Who We Are

General Federal Industries LLC was formed in California in January 2026 as a founder-led contracting company with a wide brief and a single standard: serve the public well, and put Californians to work doing it.

Our founder's experience was earned firsthand in disaster zones — including fire response work in the Palisades — and in a career spent around safety, service, and creative production. Technology in Service of the Mission

Technology is not our decoration; it is our method. Every General Federal Industries engagement runs on systems we build and use ourselves:

  • AI-assisted operations. We use artificial intelligence the way a good crew uses power tools — to draft, organize, schedule, and check work faster, with human judgment on every decision that counts.
  • Live dashboards and monitoring. Work status, quality checks, and service data are tracked on live dashboards, so problems surface early and performance is visible — not buried in a monthly report.
  • Documented workflows. Our processes are written down, step by step, and run the same way every time — so quality does not depend on who happens to be on shift.
  • Secure digital intake. Clients send work requests through our secure sign-in portal, and every document attached to a request is malware-scanned before anyone on our team can open it.

The goal is simple: technology should make public service faster, safer, and more accountable — and put human effort where only humans can do the work.

Our Product — GeneralOS

GeneralOS is the business platform we built for ourselves — and now offer to the world. It helps small and large businesses everywhere run their customers, sales pipeline, and follow-up in one simple place, with AI agent bots inside doing the everyday work.

  • AI that does the work — with your approval. GeneralOS's built-in agents draft follow-ups, organize contacts, and prepare the next step on their own. Nothing is ever sent or finalized until a person on your team looks it over and approves it — your business stays in control, always.
  • Your brand, not ours. Companies run GeneralOS under their own name, logo, and colors. Your customers see your business; the platform stays invisible.
  • Simple enough for anyone. GeneralOS is built so simply that anyone on the team can run the company on it — no training courses, no consultants, no manual the size of a phone book.
  • Built by operators, for operators. We run General Federal Industries on GeneralOS ourselves. It was made by people who do real work, for people who do real work — from a two-person shop to a company operating around the world.

GeneralOS is now launching. If your business is ready for a system that works as hard as you do, talk to us.
