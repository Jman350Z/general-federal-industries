Capabilities Statement

General Federal Industries LLC is a California government contracting company that uses technology to push humanity forward. This statement summarizes our core competencies, differentiators, service area, and company information for contracting officers, procurement staff, and teaming partners.

Core Competencies

  • Disaster Response & Recovery Support — damage assessment support, debris and remediation support services, and program support for response and recovery operations. Our founder brings firsthand experience working in disaster zones, including fire response in the Palisades; that field experience informs how we plan, staff, and document support work. It is offered as services capability — stated plainly, without embellishment.
  • Facilities Services — contract janitorial and custodial programs and day-to-day building operations for government offices and public facilities: scheduled daily, weekly, and periodic cleaning performed to written specification; restroom sanitation and restocking; floor care for hard floors and carpeted areas; trash and recycling handling; and on-call response for unscheduled facility needs as contracts require.
  • Technology Services — AI-assisted business systems, data dashboards and monitoring, and digital workflow setup for agencies and businesses: practical implementations that replace paper processes, surface performance data in real time, and give managers clear oversight of operations. Our flagship product is GeneralOS, our AI-powered business platform — built to help small and large businesses around the world run customers, sales, and follow-up in one simple place, with AI agents that do the everyday work under human approval.
  • Alarm & Life-Safety Monitoring — fire alarm and life-safety monitoring arrangements for public facilities, coordinated with licensed monitoring partners.
  • Environmental & Site Services — grounds care, site cleanup support, scheduled pest control services, and facility supply programs (paper, chemicals, and consumables) that keep public sites clean, safe, and presentable.
  • Creative & Media Services — digital content, publishing, and design services produced in-house, from public-safety education materials to the books, media, and content programs we produce under our own name.

Differentiators

  • Technology-forward operations. Our work runs on AI-assisted systems, live dashboards, and documented digital workflows — the same systems we build for clients — so performance is visible and quality is consistent.
  • Secure client intake. Work requests come through our secure client sign-in portal, and every attached document is malware-scanned before anyone on our team can open it. Procurement paperwork should never be an attack surface.
  • Founder-led accountability. The people who sign the contract are the people accountable for its performance. Questions reach the owner, not a call center.
  • Field-grounded leadership. Our founder has worked firsthand in disaster zones, including the Palisades fire response. We understand the environments we support because we have stood in them.
  • California-based, California workforce. We hire where we serve — local people, local knowledge, and wages that stay in the community.
  • Documented quality assurance. Work is performed to written specification, supervised, verified, and recorded; deficiencies are reported honestly and corrected promptly.

How We Perform

We staff every engagement as scheduled and perform work to the contract's written specification. Supervision verifies completed work against documented checklists, and any deficiency is reported and corrected promptly. Our crews are trained for the environments they serve — including site safety awareness, chemical handling, and bloodborne-pathogen precautions where the work requires it.

Behind the field work, our office runs on the same technology discipline we sell: digital workflows for scheduling and reporting, dashboards that keep status visible, and records kept as if an auditor will read them — because one might.

Service Area & Sectors Served

General Federal Industries LLC is based in Yucca Valley and serves the State of California. We pursue contracting opportunities with U.S. federal agencies, California state agencies, and local and municipal governments, and we provide the same services to commercial clients.

Our Mission

General Federal Industries was founded to do two things well: serve the public with dependable contracting work across the full arc of our capabilities — from art to disaster zones — and provide employment opportunities across California. Technology is our method; people are the point. Every engagement we perform builds stronger, more resilient communities and puts local Californians to work in them.
