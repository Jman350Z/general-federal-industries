Capabilities Statement

General Federal Industries LLC provides janitorial and facilities services to government facilities throughout California. This statement summarizes our services, service area, and company information for contracting officers, procurement staff, and teaming partners.

Core Services

  • Contract janitorial services — scheduled daily, weekly, and periodic cleaning of government offices and public facilities, performed to written specification.
  • Restroom sanitation and restocking — cleaning, disinfecting, and supply replenishment to maintain sanitary, presentable public restrooms.
  • Floor care — sweeping, mopping, and vacuuming of hard floors and carpeted areas as part of scheduled service programs.
  • Trash and recycling handling — routine collection and removal from offices, work areas, and public spaces.
  • Facilities support services — day-to-day support that keeps public buildings clean, orderly, and ready for use.
  • On-call and emergency cleaning response — prompt response for spills, biohazard cleanup coordination, and unscheduled facility needs as contracts require.

How We Perform

We staff every facility as scheduled and perform work to the contract's written specification. Supervision verifies completed work, and any deficiency is reported and corrected promptly. Our crews are trained for the environments they serve — including site safety awareness, chemical handling, and bloodborne-pathogen precautions where the work requires it.

As an owner-led company, the people who sign the contract are the people accountable for its performance.

Service Area

State of California. General Federal Industries LLC pursues contracting opportunities with California state agencies and performs on site at government facilities across the state.

Our Mission

General Federal Industries was founded to do two things well: serve California government facilities with dependable janitorial and facilities work, and provide employment opportunities across California. Every contract we perform puts local Californians to work in the communities our clients serve.
